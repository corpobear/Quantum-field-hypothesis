**Status:** speculative paper addendum; not established physics  
**Author:** Adrian Newton / corpobear  
**Version:** v0.13

## Abstract

This addendum tests a dark-manifest extension of the eight-sector knot/shadow model. Visible-manifest matter is modeled as a one-sector axial tip intake. Dark-manifest matter is modeled as a six-sector lateral side intake with suppressed light/electromagnetic activation but preserved mass and gravitational projection. The reduced side/tip aperture ratio is compared with the Planck 2018 dark-to-baryonic matter density ratio.

---

## 1. Motivation

v0.12 separated activation into visibility, mass capture, gravitational projection, and knot coherence. This allows a dark-manifest category:

$$
L_i\approx0,\quad H_i>0,\quad G_i>0,\quad K_i>0.
$$

v0.13 proposes a geometry for this sector using the eight-sector knot/shadow structure.

## 2. Geometry

Use:

$$
C=8.
$$

The eight sectors are split as:

```text
1 sector  = visible axial tip
1 sector  = opposite axis
6 sectors = lateral side-intake belt
```

Thus:

$$
N_{side}=6.
$$

## 3. Visible aperture

The visible aperture is:

$$
A_{tip}=\frac{1}{56}+\frac{7}{8}\frac{1}{448}\langle O_\varphi\rangle.
$$

With:

$$
\langle O_\varphi\rangle=0.9837806705,
$$

this gives:

$$
A_{tip}=0.01977858948.
$$

## 4. Dark side aperture

The dark-manifest side aperture is:

$$
A_{side}=6\left(\frac{1}{56}\right)=0.1071428571.
$$

The reduced ratio is:

$$
\frac{A_{side}}{A_{tip}}=5.417.
$$

$$
\Omega_c h^2\approx0.120,

$$
\frac{\Omega_c}{\Omega_b}\approx5.357.
