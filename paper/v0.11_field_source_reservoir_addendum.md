## Abstract

This addendum extends the Fibonacci-Higgs anchor-tip source model by adding a bounded fourth-mode reservoir availability term. The fourth-mode sector is treated as a large unmanifest amplitude supply, but not as a raw mass multiplier. The field-source equation is tested in reduced integrated form by collapsing the finite anchor-tip source over its normalized core. In the tau-like reduced test, saturated reservoir availability, optimal funnel capture, and the v0.9 Fibonacci-Higgs resonance overlap reproduce the target tau value used in the prior toy reports.

## 2. Bounded reservoir availability

Define:

```math
R_4^{gate}=\frac{R_4}{R_4+R_*}.
```

This keeps:

```math
0\le R_4^{gate}\le1.
```

If the fourth-mode supply is small:

```math
R_4\ll R_* \Rightarrow R_4^{gate}\approx0.
```

If the fourth-mode supply is very large:

```math
R_4\gg R_* \Rightarrow R_4^{gate}\approx1.
```

Thus a large reservoir means the source is available; it does not imply infinite mass.
