# v0.7 Paper Addendum — One-Point Shadow Anchoring from Vibration-Motion Merger

**Status:** speculative toy-model addendum; not established physics  
**Purpose:** integrate the v0.7 shadow-anchor derivation into the paper-level theory narrative.

---

## Abstract

The previous MCIFT shadow-projection model used a local tau echo fraction of `7/8`, motivated by the tau-like third mode having eight information sectors. This addendum proposes a deeper derivation. A shadow must touch its origin at least once to remain correlated, but two or more contact points create vibrating bridges. In MCIFT, vibration opens exchange channels, directional exchange creates motion, and origin-directed motion begins merging the shadow back into the source. Therefore a stable shadow is forced to use the smallest nonzero contact count: one shared point. For an eight-sector tau knot, one shared point leaves seven independent shadow sectors, giving the `7/8` echo.

---

## 1. The problem

The tau-like third mode is modeled as an eight-sector knot:

$$
C_3=2^3=8.
$$

The shadow-projection correction used:

$$
f_0=\frac{7}{8}.
$$

Earlier this could be read as a fitted-looking local fraction. The v0.7 goal is to explain why it is geometrically forced.

The question is:

> Why should an eight-sector knot have a seven-sector independent shadow echo?

The proposed answer is:

> Because the shadow shares exactly one vibrating anchor point with the original knot.

---

## 2. Original knot, shadow knot, and contact count

Let the original eight-sector knot be `K_8` and its shadow be `S_8`.

Define the number of shared origin-shadow contact points:

$$
k=|K_8\cap S_8|.
$$

The independent shadow echo fraction is:

$$
B_8(k)=\frac{8-k}{8}.
$$

If stable geometry forces `k=1`, then:

$$
B_8(1)=\frac{8-1}{8}=\frac{7}{8}.
$$

Thus the key task is not calculating the fraction after `k=1`. The key task is proving why `k=1` is the stable contact number.

---

## 3. Connection requirement

A disconnected shadow is not a shadow of the origin. It is an unanchored duplicate.

If:

$$
k=0,
$$

then:

$$
K_8\cap S_8=\varnothing.
$$

There is no channel gate connecting shadow and origin.

Therefore a true shadow requires:

$$
k\ge1.
$$

This is the connection requirement.

---

## 4. Bridge-count geometry

A single contact point creates an anchor but does not create a bridge.

Two contact points create the first bridge.

For `k` shared contact points, the number of pairwise contact bridges is:

$$
M(k)=\frac{k(k-1)}{2}.
$$

So:

$$
M(0)=0,
$$

$$
M(1)=0,
$$

$$
M(2)=1,
$$

$$
M(3)=3.
$$

Thus the first bridge appears at `k=2`.

---

## 5. Vibration makes bridges active

MCIFT assumes that vibration opens information-exchange channels when information states, frequencies, and phases align:

$$
\Gamma_{ij}
=
g
\exp\left[-\frac{(I_i-I_j)^2}{2\sigma_I^2}\right]
\exp\left[-\frac{(\omega_i-\omega_j)^2}{2\sigma_\omega^2}\right]
\cos^2(\phi_i-\phi_j).
$$

For two origin-shadow contact points, define bridge resonance:

$$
R_{ab}=\Gamma_a\Gamma_b\cos^2(\phi_a-\phi_b).
$$

If the contact phases align, the bridge becomes strong:

$$
\phi_a\approx\phi_b
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
R_{ab}\ \text{large}.
$$

Therefore two contact points are not merely two anchors. They are a possible vibrating bridge.

---

## 6. Directional exchange creates merger-motion

In the motion sector of MCIFT, directional exchange creates velocity:

$$
\vec{\Gamma}_n=\frac{1}{C_n}\sum_{i<j}\Gamma_{ij}\vec{d}_{ij}
$$

and:

$$
\beta_n=\frac{v_n}{c_*}=\tanh(\eta|\vec{\Gamma}_n|).
$$

For the shadow problem, define merger-motion as the component of motion directed from the shadow toward the origin:

$$
v_{\mathrm{merge}}(k)
\propto
\sum_{a<b}^{k} R_{ab}.
$$

If `k=1`, there are no contact pairs:

$$
v_{\mathrm{merge}}(1)=0.
$$

If `k=2`, there is one pair:

$$
v_{\mathrm{merge}}(2)\propto R_{12}>0
$$

whenever the bridge resonates.

Thus `k=2` begins origin-shadow merger.

---

## 7. Minimal stable shadow-anchor theorem

A stable shadow must satisfy two constraints:

$$
k\ge1
$$

and:

$$
v_{\mathrm{merge}}(k)=0.
$$

Since merger-motion begins when pairwise bridges exist, no merger requires:

$$
M(k)=\frac{k(k-1)}{2}=0.
$$

This allows:

$$
k=0
$$

or:

$$
k=1.
$$

But `k=0` violates the connection requirement.

Therefore:

$$
k_*=1.
$$

This is the minimal stable shadow-anchor theorem.

---

## 8. Tau result

For the tau-like third mode:

$$
C_3=8.
$$

The stable contact count is:

$$
k_*=1.
$$

Therefore:

$$
f_0=B_8(1)=\frac{8-1}{8}=\frac{7}{8}.
$$

The proof chain is:

```text
shadow must be connected -> k >= 1
stable shadow must not merge -> M(k)=0
M(k)=0 gives k=0 or k=1
k=0 is disconnected
therefore k=1
C_3=8 with k=1 -> 7/8
```

---

## 9. Interpretation

The shadow is not empty absence. It is a channel-making companion geometry.

One point of contact identifies the origin and opens the channel gate. Seven remaining sectors echo independently.

Two points of contact over-identify the origin. They define a vibrating bridge. Once vibration activates the bridge, directional exchange creates motion along it. The shadow then begins to merge back into the original rather than remain an independent echo.

In compact form:

```text
one point = anchor
two points = bridge
bridge + vibration = directional exchange
directional exchange = motion
motion toward origin = merger
```

This addendum is an internal derivation for the MCIFT toy model. It does not establish the model as real particle physics. It makes the internal logic sharper by turning the `7/8` tau shadow echo into a consequence of a minimal stable contact condition.
