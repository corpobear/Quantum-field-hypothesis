# Paper Addendum v0.12: Activation Terminology and Dark-Manifest Matter

**Status:** speculative terminology addendum; not established physics  
**Author:** Adrian Newton / corpobear  
**Version:** v0.12

---

## Abstract

This addendum clarifies the meaning of activation in MCIFT. Earlier shorthand described light as an activation channel, but this should not be read as saying that light is the permission for existence itself. In v0.12, light activation means electromagnetic or visibility-channel activation. Mass capture, gravitational projection, and knot coherence are separate channel conditions. This allows the model to distinguish unmanifest information from dark-manifest, mass-bearing but light-inactive knots.

---

## 1. Corrected terminology

Do not use:

```text
light activation = existence itself
```

Use:

```text
light activation = electromagnetic / visibility-channel activation
```

The absence of light activation means lack of visible or electromagnetic manifestation. It does not automatically mean lack of mass, gravity, or knot coherence.

---

## 2. Channel-separated activation

Use separate activation factors:

$$
L_i=\text{light / electromagnetic visibility activation}
$$

$$
H_i=\text{Higgs / mass-capture activation}
$$

$$
G_i=\text{gravitational projection}
$$

$$
K_i=\text{knot coherence}
$$

Then:

$$
\mathrm{Visibility}_i=L_iK_i
$$

$$
\mathrm{Mass}_i=H_iK_i
$$

$$
\mathrm{Gravity}_i=G_iH_iK_i
$$

This makes manifestation channel-dependent instead of a single yes/no state.

---

## 3. Matter-state terminology

### Unmanifest

$$
L_i\approx0,\quad H_i\approx0,\quad G_i\approx0,\quad K_i\approx0
$$

No stable visible, mass, or gravitational projection.

### Visible-manifest

$$
L_i>0,\quad H_i>0,\quad G_i>0,\quad K_i>0
$$

Visible/electromagnetic, mass-bearing, gravity-projecting, and knot-coherent.

### Dark-manifest

$$
L_i\approx0,\quad H_i>0,\quad G_i>0,\quad K_i>0
$$

Mass-bearing and gravity-projecting, but light-inactive or electromagnetically suppressed.

This is the MCIFT terminology for dark-matter-like behavior.

---

## 4. Corrected rule

Old shorthand:

$$
L_i=0\Rightarrow M_i=0
$$

Corrected v0.12 rule:

$$
L_i=0\Rightarrow \mathrm{Visibility}_i=0
$$

Mass depends on:

$$
H_iK_i
$$

Gravity depends on:

$$
G_iH_iK_i.
$$

---

## 5. Consequence for dark matter language

In MCIFT language, dark-matter-like structure should not be called unmanifest. It should be called dark-manifest if it has mass and gravitational projection but lacks ordinary light-channel activation.

Compact statement:

```text
dark-manifest matter = mass-active and gravity-active knots with suppressed light/electromagnetic activation
```

---

## 6. Relationship to v0.11 source equation

The v0.11 source equation describes mass capture through a bounded reservoir, Higgs overlap, and funnel-speed window. v0.12 clarifies that this mass capture does not require the light/visibility channel to be active.

Thus a knot can satisfy:

$$
H_i>0,\quad G_i>0,\quad K_i>0
$$

while still having:

$$
L_i\approx0.
$$

That is the dark-manifest sector.
