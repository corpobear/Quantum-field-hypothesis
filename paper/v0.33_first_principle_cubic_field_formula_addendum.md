**Status:** speculative paper-style first-principle formulation; not established physics.  
**Scope:** combines the cube-center six-connector geometry, cube-face Higgs vortex mass mechanism, and collapse-containment rule into one MCIFT field formula.

---

## 1. Purpose

v0.33 turns the cubic model into a field object.

The MCIFT field is not treated as only a scalar at a point. It is represented as a cell-complex field with:

```text
node variables at cube centers
connector variables on center-to-center links
face variables on Higgs-coupled cube planes
cell variables for mass, complexity, stability, and collapse reservoir
```

## 2. Primitive structure

Cube centers are indexed by:

```text
i in Z^3
```

The six connector directions are:

```text
D = {+x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z}
```

The neighboring cell in direction `mu` is:

```text

```

## 4. Connector agreement

Each connector activation obeys:

```text

```


## 5. Information compatibility

A connector creates a vortex only when the center-to-center information relation is compatible:

```text

         * P_phase(i,j)
         * P_timing(i,j)
         * P_match(i,j)
```

with:

```text
P_phase  = cos^2(phi_i - phi_j)
P_timing = exp[-(T_i - T_j)^2 / tau_0^2]
P_match  = exp[-|K_i - K_j|^2 / sigma_K^2]
```

## 6. Higgs face-plane vortex

Each connector crosses a Higgs-coupled cube face:

```text

```

The vortex strength is:


where:

```text

```

Total mass gathered by the knot:

## 8. Coherence, complexity, and collapse

Mean connector activation:

```text

```

Directional imbalance:

```text
Delta_i = sqrt[(a_i,+x-a_i,-x)^2
             + (a_i,+y-a_i,-y)^2
             + (a_i,+z-a_i,-z)^2]
```

Connector coherence capacity:

```text

```

Contained complexity:

```text
q_i = q_i,base + alpha_m m_i + alpha_Omega sum_mu |grad_mu Omega_i,mu|
```

Containment score:

```text
S_i = Coh_i - q_i
```

Collapse-reservoir update:

```text
dB_i/dt = gamma_B max(0,-S_i) - decay_B B_i
```
