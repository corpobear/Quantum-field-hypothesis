# Antimatter Channel Geometry v0.15

**Project:** Multi-Channel Information Field Theory / Quantum-field-hypothesis  
**Status:** speculative toy-model extension; not established physics  
**Purpose:** define antimatter as positive-mass channel-reversed geometry, not negative mass.

---

## 1. Correction to the naive sign idea

Do not model antimatter as negative mass.

Use:

```text
antimatter = positive mass with reversed charge / phase / channel orientation
```

Do not use:

```text
antimatter = negative gravitational mass
```

This follows the existing MCIFT sign convention used for dark-manifest source orientation: a negative sign marks inverse field orientation, while mass density uses magnitude.

---

## 2. Matter channel geometry

The ordinary matter geometry keeps the previous visible/dark split:

```text
visible/light drill: axial tip intake
dark side sink: lateral side intake
```

A compact source sketch is:

$$
S_M = +\lambda_M \Omega_M W_M A_M R_4^{gate}\delta_M.
$$

Mass density uses magnitude:

$$
\rho_M \propto |S_M|.
$$

---

## 3. Antimatter channel adjustment

The antimatter geometry is not just the matter geometry with a minus sign.

It reverses the routing:

```text
antimatter dark sink:
  pulls Higgs-response drops directly from the Higgs/mass channel,
  not from the six lateral side-intake belt.

antimatter light drill:
  discards the central axial intake,
  and pulls in the surrounding light-channel splashes instead.
```

Interpretation:

```text
matter visible drill      = center-tip collector
matter dark sink          = side-belt collector
antimatter dark sink      = Higgs-drop collector
antimatter light drill    = splash-ring collector with discarded middle
```

## 4. Antimatter source sketch

Let the Higgs-drop aperture be:

$$
A_{Hdrop}.
$$

Let the splash-ring light aperture be:

$$
A_{splash}.
$$

Let the discarded middle aperture be:

$$
A_{mid}.
$$

Then the antimatter dark/mass source can be written as:

$$
S_{\bar M,dark}
=
-\lambda_{\bar M,H}
\Omega_{\bar M}
W_{Hdrop}
A_{Hdrop}
R_4^{gate}
\delta_{Hdrop}.
$$

The antimatter light/visible source can be written as:

$$
S_{\bar M,light}
=
-\lambda_{\bar M,L}
\Omega_{\bar M}
W_{splash}
(A_{splash}-A_{mid})
R_4^{gate}
\delta_{splash}.
$$

The negative sign denotes reversed antimatter channel orientation, not negative mass.

Mass density remains positive:

$$
\rho_{\bar M}\propto |S_{\bar M,dark}|+|S_{\bar M,light}|.
$$

## 5. Matter-antimatter meeting rule

Matter and antimatter cancel only in the matched channel-overlap part.

Define the matched annihilation overlap:

$$
O_{M\bar M}
=
O_{phase}
O_{charge}
O_{geometry}.
$$

Because the antimatter routing differs from the matter routing, total cancellation does not automatically follow from equal mass alone.

A toy residual term can be represented as:

$$
\rho_{rem}
=
\left|
\rho_M-\rho_{\bar M}^{matched}
\right|
+
\rho_{\bar M}^{unmatched},
$$

where:

```text
matched      = channel geometry overlaps matter and antimatter cleanly
unmatched    = Higgs-drop or splash-ring structure not paired by the matter geometry
```

For ordinary known annihilation, the residual should normally be zero or extremely small unless the early-universe environment creates a formation/survival asymmetry.

## 6. Antimatter asymmetry target

The useful question becomes:

```text
Does the Higgs-drop / splash-ring antimatter routing create a small survival-efficiency difference between matter and antimatter?
```

Define:

$$
\epsilon_{MCIFT}
=
\frac{\Gamma_M-\Gamma_{\bar M}}{\Gamma_M+\Gamma_{\bar M}}.
$$

Possible contributors:

```text
Higgs-drop capture efficiency
splash-ring light-channel collection efficiency
middle-discard loss term
phase-orientation mismatch
reservoir-gate timing
vortex survival stability
```

Matter remains after early annihilation only if:

$$
\epsilon_{MCIFT}>0.
$$

## 7. Falsifiability requirements

This model is only useful if future versions derive or constrain:

```text
A_Hdrop
A_splash
A_mid
W_Hdrop
W_splash
O_geometry
epsilon_MCIFT
```

Without those quantities, the antimatter geometry remains a metaphor.

With those quantities, the model can be checked against antimatter mass positivity, annihilation behavior, baryon asymmetry constraints, and collider bounds.
