# Activation Terminology v0.12

**Status:** terminology clarification; speculative toy-model language  
**Purpose:** clarify that light activation means electromagnetic/visible activation, not existence itself.

---

## 1. Core correction

Previous shorthand sometimes implied:

```text
no light activation -> no manifestation / no mass
```

The corrected terminology is:

```text
no light activation -> no visible or electromagnetic manifestation
```

This does not automatically mean:

```text
no mass
no gravity
no stable knot
no existence
```

In MCIFT language, light should be treated as the visibility/electromagnetic channel, not the only permission for reality.

---

## 2. Separate activation channels

Use separate channel factors:

$$
L_i = \text{light / electromagnetic visibility activation}
$$

$$
H_i = \text{Higgs / mass-capture activation}
$$

$$
G_i = \text{gravitational projection}
$$

$$
K_i = \text{knot coherence}
$$

Then:

$$
\mathrm{Visibility}_i=L_iK_i
$$

$$
\mathrm{Mass}_i=H_iK_i
$$

$$
\mathrm{Gravity}_i=G_iH_iK_i
$$

This prevents the model from confusing invisibility with nonexistence.

---

## 3. Matter classes

### Unmanifest reservoir

$$
L_i\approx0,\quad H_i\approx0,\quad G_i\approx0,\quad K_i\approx0
$$

Interpretation:

```text
not visibly activated
not mass-captured
not gravitationally projected
not a stable manifested knot
```

### Visible matter

$$
L_i>0,\quad H_i>0,\quad G_i>0,\quad K_i>0
$$

Interpretation:

```text
electromagnetically visible
mass-bearing
gravity-projecting
stable knot
```

### Dark-manifest matter

$$
L_i\approx0,\quad H_i>0,\quad G_i>0,\quad K_i>0
$$

Interpretation:

```text
not light-active / not electromagnetically visible
mass-bearing
gravity-projecting
stable knot
```

This is the MCIFT-friendly place to discuss dark-matter-like behavior.

---

## 4. Updated language rule

Do not say:

```text
Light is the permission event for existence.
```

Use:

```text
Light is the visibility-channel activation event.
```

Do not say:

```text
If light does not activate a point, it has no mass.
```

Use:

```text
If light does not activate a point, it does not enter the visible/electromagnetic channel. It may still be mass-bearing if Higgs/mass capture and gravitational projection are active.
```

---

## 5. Corrected manifestation rule

The old shorthand was:

$$
L_i=0\Rightarrow M_i=0.
$$

The corrected rule is:

$$
L_i=0\Rightarrow \mathrm{Visibility}_i=0.
$$

Mass instead depends on:

$$
H_iK_i.
$$

Gravity depends on:

$$
G_iH_iK_i.
$$

So the broad manifestation state is channel-dependent, not a single yes/no property.

---

## 6. Practical terminology

Use these terms going forward:

```text
light-active / visible-active
  participates in electromagnetic or visible manifestation

mass-active
  participates in Higgs/funnel mass capture

gravity-active
  projects gravitationally

unmanifest
  no stable visible, mass, or gravitational projection

dark-manifest
  mass-active and gravity-active, but light-inactive

visible-manifest
  light-active, mass-active, gravity-active, and knot-coherent
```

---

## 7. Why this matters

This terminology allows MCIFT to discuss dark-matter-like behavior without saying dark matter is unmanifest.

A dark-matter-like knot should be described as:

```text
manifest through mass and gravity,
but not manifest through light/electromagnetic activation.
```

That keeps the theory from making the overly strong claim that lack of light activation means lack of mass.
