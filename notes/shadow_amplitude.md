# Shadow Amplitude Extension

**Status:** speculative toy-model extension, v0.2.

This note introduces a mathematical shadow term for the information cluster. The word shadow means an inverted or complementary information pattern, not an established physical particle.

## Core idea

For every visible information cluster:

$$
I_n
$$

introduce a complementary inverted pattern:

$$
I_n^s
$$

The full knot state is:

$$
K_n=(I_n,I_n^s)
$$

The relevant quantity is the imbalance between the visible pattern and the complementary pattern.

## Amplitude first

Define the mass amplitude:

$$
A_n=I_n-I_n^s
$$

Then mass is modeled as:

$$
m_n=A_n^2
$$

This is useful because charged-lepton mass relations such as Koide's relation naturally use square-root masses:

$$
A_n=\sqrt{m_n}
$$

## Correction rule

Let the previous MCIFT toy model give a base mass:

$$
m_n^{base}
$$

Then:

$$
A_n^{base}=\sqrt{m_n^{base}}
$$

Introduce an amplitude correction:

$$
\Delta_n
$$

so that:

$$
A_n=A_n^{base}\Delta_n
$$

and:

$$
m_n=m_n^{base}\Delta_n^2
$$

## Meaning

A small amplitude correction can produce a larger mass correction after squaring.

In the charged-lepton toy test, the old MCIFT tau prediction was low by about 3.995 percent in mass. In amplitude space, the needed correction is only about 1.978 percent.

## Koide connection

Koide's charged-lepton relation can be written as:

$$
\frac{A_1^2+A_2^2+A_3^2}{(A_1+A_2+A_3)^2}=\frac{2}{3}
$$

where:

$$
A_i=\sqrt{m_i}
$$

This suggests that MCIFT should try to derive the geometry of the amplitude vector:

$$
(\sqrt{m_e},\sqrt{m_\mu},\sqrt{m_\tau})
$$

rather than fitting the final masses directly.

## Best summary

**Mass may be modeled as the squared imbalance between a visible information cluster and its complementary inverted pattern.**

## Caveat

This is a speculative mathematical extension. It is not a claim of established physics. The next task is to derive the amplitude correction from knot geometry rather than choosing it after observing the tau mass.
